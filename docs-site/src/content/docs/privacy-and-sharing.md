---
title: Privacy and sharing
description: What stays yours, what you can share with a coach, and how to see who looked.
---

Your rides are private unless you share them. Your club always sees your name and your place in the roster, and nothing else until you turn something on.

## What you share

*Profile, Ride privacy* and the sharing choices let you turn on, one by one:

| Choice | What a coach with your grant sees |
|--------|-----------------------------------|
| **Rides** | Every number and stream of the rides you share (power, heart rate, speed, cadence, elevation, temperature) and the training built from them. They only read; your rides stay yours to change or delete. |
| **Routes** | The map. Without it, a shared ride keeps every signal except latitude and longitude. |
| **Wellbeing** | Your check-in scores and how hard each ride felt (RPE). |

Your own notes, suggestions and effort score stay yours. You can stop sharing at any time.

**Wellbeing is health data.** Velomine asks for explicit consent before the first check-in is saved. Free-text notes are never shown to a coach, and your readiness score is only for you.

A club never reads your data through membership alone, and a club admin gets nothing from their role: access is you, or a grant you gave.

## Hiding places

*Ride privacy* sets what your coach and shared links never see of your routes. You always see everything.

- **Hide the start and end.** 0 to 5000 metres at each end of every ride.
- **Saved places.** Up to ten places, each with a radius from 200 to 1609 metres. Every ride hides the area around them, and the route line breaks there.

These apply even when you share Routes.

## Who looked

Every time a coach opens something you shared, it is recorded, and you can read the log. Rides show "Seen by" with who and when.

## Take it with you

You can export everything, including every original ride file, or delete your account and everything with it, whenever you want. Delete means delete: rides, files and derived data. *Profile, Your data* has the export, and *Delete my account* is in the `⋯` menu there.

On a self-hosted server your data lives where your club runs it.
