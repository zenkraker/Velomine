---
title: Clubs and coaches
description: Join or start a club, plan rides, and what a coach can see.
---

You can ride on your own and join a club whenever you want. You can leave any time, and your rides stay yours.

## Joining

*Club* gives three ways in, side by side:

- **With a code.** The owner shares a join code or a link; open it and you are in.
- **Clubs near you.** Sorted by distance, from your position or a saved location. Your position is used only to sort the list and is not kept on the server. *Ask to join* sends a request, with an optional word for the owner. You can have one open request; asking another club withdraws it.
- **Start a club.** A name, a location chosen on the map, and whether riders nearby can find it. On a self-hosted server this is available when `VELOMINE__ACCOUNTS__CLUBCREATIONENABLED` is on.

The owner decides each request. A new member joins as a rider.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|------|-----|
| **Member** | See the club's rides and routes, join rides, share what they choose. |
| **Coach** | Also plan rides and read what athletes share with them. |
| **Admin** | Also manage members and their roles. |

The owner makes a new join code from the `⋯` menu and cannot leave. A club admin gets no access to a rider's data from their role.

## Club rides and routes

- **Club rides** is the calendar: each ride has a date, meeting time and place, and a pace, and shows whether you are in, it is full, or it is cancelled. Owners and coaches plan rides.
- **Routes** is a shared library with the line drawn on the map. Filter by surface and sort.
- **Calendar subscription** gives a private address your calendar app can follow. The address is shown once, and it is a secret: replacing it ends the old one, and *Stop* removes it.

## Coaches and athletes

A coach sees only what each rider shares, and only to read. *Athletes* lists the members who share with you; opening one shows chips for what is shared and a card for each part: rides, training, routes, wellbeing (scores and RPE, never notes). With Rides shared and auto training on, a **Plan** card shows the goal and this week's sessions, planned beside done.

Everything you open is recorded for the rider, who can read it. See [Privacy and sharing](/docs/privacy-and-sharing/).
