Early access

Be told when the cloud opens.

The Velomine cloud is not open yet. Today you can try the demo or self-host it. Leave an address and you get one email when the cloud opens. If you say your club might try it, you also get a reply about a pilot.

There is no account and no newsletter. Your answers are emailed to whoever runs this server and are not stored in Velomine’s database. They are deleted on request, and once the cloud has opened and the announcement is sent. See the privacy page.
