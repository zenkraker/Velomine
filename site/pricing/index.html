A few honest answers.

How does the pricing work?

A yearly fee for your club’s size band: €190 up to 25 members, €390 up to 75, €690 up to 150 and €990 above that, before VAT. It is never counted per member, and clubs of the same size pay the same. Guided setup is an optional €200, paid once. There is no feature paywall.

What counts as a club, and how is its size set?

Any group that rides together: five friends or a federated society. There is one kind of club and one price list. The band is the most members a club can have: a club in the first band can accept up to 25. To accept more, it moves up a band and pays the difference for the rest of its year. Inside a band, accepting someone never changes the bill, and a member of a paying club pays nothing.

Is self-hosting really free?

Yes, for riders and clubs, under AGPL-3.0. Every feature is included and there is no member limit. If Velomine is useful to you, an optional supporter licence helps keep it going; it unlocks nothing.

Do I need to be technical?

Self-hosted: guided setup covers the installation; the club provides the machine and backups. Cloud: nothing to run. Either way you do not have to build the app.

Is there a hosted version?

Yes. Our managed cloud in the EU, with every club’s data kept apart, alongside self-hosting. We run the app, updates and backups. New-club enrollment is done with you personally while the onboarding automation is finished.

Can I move my data?

Yes. Download a ZIP with your profile, bikes, goals, rides and every original ride file. You can also delete the account and everything in it; backups expire within 90 days. Strava export import works today, alongside FIT, GPX and TCX files. The solo plan is not open yet: solo sign-up and payment are still being built.
