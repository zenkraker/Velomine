Status

What works today.

Velomine is in early development. This page states the v1 goal, lists what is implemented and usable today, and then what is still missing.

The v1 goal

Move in, ride, belong.

Bring your whole riding history with you, see every ride with its full analysis, and ride with your club. No feature sits behind a payment, and your data stays yours.

Move in Ride Belong

What is in the app.

Everything below is implemented and usable today. The next section lists what is not.

Accounts and access

  • Sign-up with club admin approval
  • Password sign-in, with a cookie on the web and a refresh token on the phone
  • Single sign-on with the club’s existing provider
  • Password reset by email, or a one-time link from a club admin
  • Optional two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app and single-use recovery codes
  • A device list: see every signed-in phone and browser, sign one out, or all the others
  • Club admin, member and coach roles
  • Lock or delete an account; deleting removes every ride and file
  • Profile: FTP, weight, maximum heart rate, time zone and week start
  • Download every ride and file as a ZIP

Getting rides in

  • FIT, GPX and TCX uploads, including gzipped files
  • Files or a whole folder dropped on the web app, and a ride file shared or opened into the Android app
  • Strava export import: the archive is read on the server, one ride per activity
  • A hidden duplicate when the same ride arrives twice, even from another app

Rides and analysis

  • A title and a description on a ride, in your own words
  • Search the ride list by words in a title or description, and pick a date range
  • Distance, moving time, climbing, speed, power, normalized power, heart rate and cadence
  • Route map and a chart for every signal
  • Heart-rate and power zones, IF, TSS and TRIMP
  • Laps read from FIT and TCX, with numbers recomputed from the samples
  • A bike picker on every ride
  • Ride type, e-bike, indoor and commute on every ride, editable and filterable

Training and goals

  • Weekly, monthly and yearly totals
  • Goals per week, month or year: distance, climbing, riding time or number of rides
  • A 30, 90 and 365-day dashboard with daily load, fitness, fatigue and form
  • A power curve with FTP and VO2max estimates

Wellbeing

  • Daily check-in: fatigue, soreness, mood, stress and motivation
  • Consent before the first check-in, asked again when the terms change
  • RPE and notes on each ride; notes stay private
  • A readiness view from form, recent load and the check-in
  • Coaches see shared scores and RPE, never the notes

Bikes and components

  • Bikes with wheel circumference and the distance a used bike already had
  • Parts with limits by distance, riding hours or months
  • A wear bar on every part and a replace action
  • A default bike for new rides
  • A shared parts catalogue and build templates

Service and maintenance

  • A recurring reminder per job on each bike, due at the first of its distance, riding hours or months
  • Common jobs to start from, with an interval filled in; pause one without deleting it
  • A notification in the app, and by email when the server has a mail service, when a job is nearly due and when it is due, once per cycle
  • A service log with the date, what was done and, when you want, the shop, the cost and the part; the bike’s distance on that date
  • Marking a reminder done, or replacing a part, writes the log entry for you

Clubs and sharing

  • Clubs, membership and joining with a code
  • Riding without a club, finding nearby clubs that opted in, and asking to join
  • A rider without a club starts one where the server allows it: owner at once, smallest size band, 30 days to try it
  • The owner sees the club’s plan: members, the limit and the paid-until day; a full or ended plan stops new members, never the ones already in
  • Sharing rides, analytics, the route of a ride and wellbeing with coaches
  • Wellbeing only with explicit consent; notes stay private
  • Every choice is revocable
  • Read-only coach views, and a record of who looked, shown on each ride
  • Privacy zones around saved places, start and end trimming, and per-stream switches on everything a coach sees

Phone recorder

  • Android recording with the screen off
  • Standard Bluetooth LE heart-rate strap, paired once and reconnecting for later rides
  • Raw samples kept on the phone, assembled into a FIT file when you stop
  • Auto-pause, automatic upload and retries, even when the app is closed

Club admin and server

  • Every account, roles, lock, delete and club admin approval
  • Sign-up, club admin approval and the switch that lets riders start clubs
  • In-app notifications, and email when the server has a mail service: rides read or failed, parts and service nearly due, accounts waiting
  • An improved ride reader reaches stored rides: older rides are read again in the background, and the admin overview shows the version and how many still wait
  • Day and night themes, English and Italian

What is still missing.

Everything v1 needs that is not usable yet, then what comes right after it.

Promised for v1, still missing

Sign-up and payment

  • Solo sign-up with a 30-day trial
  • Payment and VAT invoices

Bike computers

  • Browsing a connected bike computer inside the app

Club

  • A ride calendar: date, meeting point, route, sign-up and a calendar feed
  • A club route library

Hosting and email

  • Database access rules per club
  • Italian emails
  • Email delivery for verification and receipts

After v1

Phone

  • Speed, cadence and power sensors
  • Push notifications

Club space

  • Coach comments on a ride
  • An opt-in feed inside the club, with reactions and comments
  • Always under each member’s own privacy settings; nothing is public
  • Ratings, notes and suggested variants on club routes

Rides and sharing

  • A shareable ride card
  • Share links
  • Photos, thumbnails and place names

Bikes and site

  • Cost per kilometre for each bike
  • Italian presentation pages under /it/
