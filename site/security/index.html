Security

Tell us before it becomes a problem.

If you find a security issue in Velomine, report it privately. We will not treat good-faith research as an attack.

How to report

Email horizontodawn@pm.me with the subject Security. Include enough to reproduce the issue: the URL or app version, the steps, the impact, and any proof that does not contain member data, health data or another person’s credentials.

If you need encryption, say so in the first email and we will arrange a key exchange. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

What is in scope

  • The Velomine server, the web app, the Android app and the presentation site.
  • Sign-in, permissions, keeping clubs’ data apart, sessions, and the export/delete flows.
  • Ways to read another club’s data, another member’s data, or a coach view without a grant.

What is out of scope

  • Third-party services a club chooses, such as its single sign-on, mail or map provider.
  • Social engineering, phishing, physical access and stolen devices.
  • Denial-of-service, spam and automated scanning that disrupts the service.
  • Reports that require a compromised admin account or a rooted device.

Safe harbour

We will not pursue legal action for good-faith research that follows these rules: do not access, change or store another person’s data beyond what is needed to demonstrate the issue; do not disrupt the service; do not publish the issue before we have had a reasonable chance to fix it; and report promptly. If in doubt, ask first.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge a report within three business days.
  • We confirm the issue, agree a fix window, and tell you when it is fixed.
  • We credit you publicly if you want, and only after the fix.
  • There is no paid bug bounty today. We will not ask you to pay for a fix.

What we do on our side

  • Encrypted traffic, role-based access, every request limited to its own club and tested against another club, and no platform admin inside a club’s app.
  • Optional two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, single-use recovery codes, and a device list where a member can sign out the others.
  • A log of every coach view of a member’s data, which the member can read.
  • The app sends crash reports and performance data only after the member opts in. The server keeps anonymised operational logs, with no member data, only to fix bugs and performance problems. The presentation site has no third-party scripts.
  • Tested backups and a restore path, with a backup window disclosed in the privacy policy.

Machine-readable contact
