Security

Report it before it becomes a problem.

If you find a security issue in Velomine, report it privately. Good-faith research is not treated as an attack.

How to report

Email horizontodawn@pm.me with the subject Security. Include enough to reproduce the issue: the URL or app version, the steps, the impact, and any proof that does not contain member data, health data or another person’s credentials.

If you need encryption, say so in the first email and a key exchange will be arranged. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

What is in scope

  • The Velomine server, the web app, the Android app and the presentation site.
  • Sign-in, permissions, keeping clubs’ data apart, sessions, and the export/delete flows.
  • Ways to read another club’s data, another member’s data, or a coach view without a grant.

What is out of scope

  • Third-party services a club chooses, such as its single sign-on, mail or map provider.
  • Social engineering, phishing, physical access and stolen devices.
  • Denial-of-service, spam and automated scanning that disrupts the service.
  • Reports that require a compromised admin account or a rooted device.

Safe harbour

Velomine will not pursue legal action for good-faith research that follows these rules: do not access, change or store another person’s data beyond what is needed to demonstrate the issue; do not disrupt the service; do not publish the issue before there has been a reasonable chance to fix it; and report promptly. If in doubt, ask first.

What happens next

  • A report is acknowledged within three business days.
  • Velomine confirms the issue, agrees a fix window, and tells you when it is fixed.
  • You are credited publicly if you want, and only after the fix.
  • There is no paid bug bounty today. You will not be asked to pay for a fix.

What Velomine does on its side

  • Encrypted traffic, role-based access, every request limited to its own club in the application, and every endpoint that touches club data tested from a second club. Per-club rules inside the database are not built yet.
  • No one at Velomine has an account inside a club’s app. Whoever runs a server can reach its database: on the cloud that is the person who runs Velomine, who does so only to fix a problem a club reports.
  • Optional two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, single-use recovery codes, and a device list where a member can sign out the others.
  • A log of every coach view of a member’s data, which the member can read.
  • The app sends crash reports and performance data only after the member opts in. The server keeps operational logs only to fix bugs and performance problems: request id, club, route template, status, duration and error code, with no names, addresses, ride or health data, kept for 30 days. The presentation site has no third-party scripts.
  • Tested backups and a restore path, with a backup window disclosed in the privacy policy.
