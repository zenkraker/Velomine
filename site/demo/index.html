Before you start

It is a sandbox, and it is honest.

Everything here is example data. Files you upload are deleted at the next reset, so do not put anything personal in it.

Changing or resetting the password, two-factor sign-in, deleting the account and changing club roles are switched off in the demo, because the accounts are shared.

What works today, and what is still missing, is kept in one place on the status page.

Five minutes in the demo

Five things worth a look.

Everything here is in the seeded club, so you can try each one with the three accounts above.

  1. Open a ride. Sign in as the rider, pick one of the rides and move over a chart: the marker follows on the map. Look at the power, heart rate, laps and zones.
  2. Check Progress. The power curve shows the best power for each duration, and training load shows fitness, fatigue and form over the weeks.
  3. Change who sees what. As the rider, open sharing and turn rides or the route off for the coach. Then sign in as the coach and see what is still there. The rider can see who looked.
  4. Look at the club. Open the club’s route library and the two planned rides, and say you are in.
  5. Tell me what broke. Anything wrong, slow or confusing is useful: send feedback.

On your phone

Record rides on your phone.

The Android app records with the screen off and uploads the ride when you stop. It is not in the Play Store yet.

Download the APK from the latest release on the releases page and install it; Android asks you to allow installing from your browser. When the app starts it asks “Which server does your club use?”. Type https://demo.velomine.eu and press Connect, then sign in with one of the demo accounts.

On an iPhone? There is no iPhone app yet: it is on the list after v1, and the monthly polls decide when it comes, so ask for it. Until then, the web app works in the iPhone’s browser (Safari is not fully tested yet, so tell me what breaks), and rides come in from a bike computer as FIT, GPX or TCX files, or from a Strava export.

Afterwards

Say what broke.

A blunt answer is worth more than a compliment. Fill in the short, anonymous feedback form and answer only what you want: what broke, what was missing, what you expected. It has no reply address, so if you want an answer, send an email instead.
