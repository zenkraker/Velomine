For the coach

One screen for the squad.

See who rode, how much load they carried and when they last trained — before Sunday. A rider’s data appears only after they share it with you, and every look is logged.

  • Last ride, distance and seven- and six-week load for every athlete.
  • A shared ride opens with its streams, laps and training load.
  • Read-only views, revocable by the rider, with a record of who looked.
  • Shared scores and RPE, never notes.

No workout plans or comments yet. Velomine is analysis and coach review today.

For the rider

Record it. Keep it. See all of it.

Record on Android with the screen off and a heart-rate strap, or add FIT, GPX and TCX files from any bike computer — one file, several, or a whole folder dropped on the web app; on Android, share or open a file into Velomine from another app. Name and describe each ride, then search your history by words or dates. Route on the map, a chart for every stream, laps, zones, TSS, the power curve, bikes, parts and service, goals and readiness.

  • Every stream on one page, with the map following the chart.
  • A name and a description on your rides, and a search over them with a date range.
  • Bikes, parts and wear, so a chain is replaced before it fails.
  • Service reminders and a log of everything done to each bike.
  • Your files stay yours: export everything, delete everything.

Android recorder today. The iOS recorder is not built yet. Heart rate only: speed, cadence and power sensors are not supported yet. Strava export import works today.

The record belongs to the rider.

Every club’s data kept apart. Members join with a code and an admin approves them; a rider without a club can start one when the server allows it, on the smallest size band with 30 days to try it. Coaches see only what each rider shares. A route library keeps the lines members add from a ride or a GPX file, each still owned by the rider who added it. A ride calendar shows the rides a coach plans, with a meeting point and a route, and members say they are in. Run it on your own server, or in our managed cloud.

Every club kept apart

Every request is limited to the club it belongs to, and tests prove one club can never see another club’s data.

Export or delete

Download everything as a ZIP, including every original file. Delete the account and all of it goes; backups expire within 90 days.

Self-host or cloud

The same software on the club’s server, or in our managed cloud in the EU. Same features, same export, same ownership.

Admin and single sign-on

Join with a code and an admin’s approval. Roles for club admin, coach and member, sign-in with the accounts the club already uses — Google, Microsoft or its own identity provider — plus optional two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, and a list of every device you are signed in on. An owner sees the club’s size band, member count and paid-until day; a full or ended plan stops new members, never the ones already in.
