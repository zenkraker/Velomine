Move in

Bring everything you have ridden.

Your history is the point, so it comes with you. Nothing is thrown away on the way in.

Any file, any bike computer

Add FIT, GPX and TCX files, one at a time or a whole folder dropped on the web app. On Android, share or open a file into Velomine from another app.

Your Strava export

Import your Strava export and every cycling activity becomes a ride. Import a newer one later and what is already here is skipped.

Nothing doubled, nothing lost

The same ride from two apps is recognised and the copy is hidden. Every original file is kept as received, so a ride can be read again when the reader improves.

Ride

Record it. Keep it. See all of it.

Record on Android with the screen off and a heart-rate strap, or add files from any bike computer. Name and describe each ride, then search your history by words or dates.

  • Every stream on one page, with the map following the chart.
  • Laps, heart-rate and power zones, TSS and the power curve.
  • Bikes, parts and wear, service reminders and a log of everything done to each bike.
  • Goals, readiness and a private wellbeing check-in.
  • Your files stay yours: export everything, delete everything.

Belong

Ride with your club. Share only what you choose.

Join a club with a code and an admin’s approval. Your rides stay yours; the coach sees what you share, read-only, and every look is logged.

  • A route library the club shares, each route still owned by the rider who added it.
  • Planned club rides with a meeting point and a route, and an “I’m in” for members.
  • A private calendar feed of the club’s rides.
  • Coaches see only what each rider shares: last ride, fatigue, fitness and form for every athlete.
  • Read-only views, revocable by the rider, with a record of who looked.

The record belongs to the rider.

Yours to export or delete

Download everything as a ZIP, including every original file. Delete the account and all of it goes; backups expire within 90 days. Nobody pays to see their own history.

Private by default

Nothing is visible to anyone until you share it, per data type, revocable, with a log of who looked. No ads, no public feed, no pro tier.

Every club kept apart

Every request is limited to its club in the application, and every endpoint that touches club data has a test from a second club.

Self-host or EU cloud, open source

The same software on your own server, or in the managed cloud in the EU. Same features, same export, same ownership. Open source under AGPL-3.0, so you can inspect it, run it and fork it.
