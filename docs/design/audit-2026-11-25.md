# UI conformance audit — 25 November 2026

The durable record of the audit of `app/lib` against `docs/design/pages/`, and of the work, the
decisions and the dead ends that came out of it. The story that ships the work is
[`docs/stories/design-conformance.md`](../stories/design-conformance.md); this file is the evidence
behind it. When a finding here is fixed, mark it ✅ and keep it: the ledger is deleted only when the
story is.

**Method.** All 45 routes over 44 screen classes, the 55 design pages (`<Page>.md` + `<Page>.html`) and all 136
widget-bearing files under `app/lib` were read: six read-only sub-audits by area, then a pass over
the systemic claims by the author. Evidence in the order it was trusted: a passing/failing test, a
screenshot from `tools/screenshots.dart`, then code read. Where a claim came from a sub-audit and was
not re-verified it is marked **A**; everything else **V**.

**Status:** ✅ done · ⬜ open · ⛔ blocked · 🔎 undiagnosed or a design-doc conflict · ⚠️ in progress

The design fixes what a chart must look like (round ticks ≤ 4, current bar `accent-strong`, past bars
`bar-muted`, 35 % → 0 gradient, four labels max, a phone label step computed from width, empty state
instead of axes). `fl_chart` can approximate most of it, not all; the ownership cost is one dependency
plus an evaluation, the saving is ~750 painter lines (`area_chart` 243, `bar_chart` 299,
`chart_chrome` 108, `goal_gauge` 112, plus 7 feature painters). **Decided (owner, 25 Nov): `fl_chart`, free (MIT), approved** — the app's charts become its widgets
behind our own `core/widgets/` APIs so the design's look still lives in one place. The evaluation doc
was waived by the owner; `AGENTS.md:222` carries the row. **The per-file work list is §6b.**
