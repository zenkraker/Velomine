# AddRoutePage

Add a route: a full-page form. Route `/club/routes/new`, `features/routes/route_forms.dart`. Once added, its name, description and surface are changed on [EditRoutePage](../routes/EditRoutePage.html) (`/club/routes/:id/edit`); the line is fixed when the route is added.

- The line comes from one of the rider's rides or a GPX file, with privacy zones already applied; the preview shows exactly what the club will see.
