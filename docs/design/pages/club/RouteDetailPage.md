# RouteDetailPage

Route: one club route. Route `/club/routes/:id`, `features/routes/route_detail_screen.dart`.

- Download GPX is the page's one action. Edit (for the member who added it, on [EditRoutePage](../routes/EditRoutePage.html)), Share or Withdraw (take it back from the club), then Delete for good (danger, confirmed) are in the `⋯` menu, for the member who added the route. A route someone else added has no menu: only its owner changes it.
