# BikeDetailPage

Bike: one bike, its parts and their wear, and its service. Route `/bikes/:id`, `features/bikes/bike_detail_screen.dart`, `service_section.dart`.

- Add part is the page's one action. Edit, build templates, Make default, Archive and Delete (danger) are in the `⋯` menu.
- Parts are grouped (drivetrain, wheels, brakes…) with their wear as a bar: `warning` when getting worn, `danger` when worn out, always with the words.
- Service reminders with Done, then the service log.
