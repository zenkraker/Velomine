# ProfilePage

Profile: the rider's numbers, preferences and account. Route `/profile`, `features/profile/profile_screen.dart`.

- Delete my account is in the `⋯` menu, last and red, confirmed with the password.
- The athlete numbers say where each came from (typed, measured on a ride, estimated). The Athlete card's Save saves only the athlete fields; the Preferences card saves each choice the moment it is made (time zone, first day of the week, language, units), with no Save of its own. Sections in cards: athlete, zones (the custom zone limits, with Back to automatic), preferences, riding (bikes, privacy, "How I ride" — the rider mode, which opens its sheet — and the switch "Auto training (experimental)" with its sentence and the feedback link: turning it on opens the training setup; turning it off is confirmed: "Your plan for this week and later goes. Your past sessions stay."), notifications, telemetry opt-in, supporter, account (password, two-factor, devices), your data.
- The supporter card shows the licence ("Supporter no. 128 · 3 Oct 2026"), a switch to hide the badge, and Play the celebration again. A solo trial shows as a badge in the same card.
