On the ride

The screen goes dark. The recording doesn't.

Start on the phone, pocket it, and ride. Velomine records GPS with the screen off, connects to a heart-rate strap, pauses when you stop, and uploads when it finds a connection.

  • FIT, GPX and TCX, or straight from the phone.
  • Heart rate, power, cadence and speed — every stream in one place.
  • Saved on the phone first, then synced to Velomine.

Recording is on Android today. Speed, cadence and power sensors are being added; iOS is next. See the status →

Sensors, without the setup

Pair the sensor. Skip the ecosystem.

Velomine pairs directly with standard Bluetooth LE heart-rate straps today. No vendor app, no extra account, no vendor cloud. Pair once in the app; the recorder reconnects for later rides, even with the screen off.

  • Heart-rate straps work today.
  • Speed, cadence and power are next.
  • The samples go straight into the ride on your club's database.

For clubs, not feeds

Private by default. Shared on purpose.

No feed. No followers. No leaderboard you didn't ask for. A member chooses what to share — rides, analytics, routes or wellbeing — with the club's coaches. Whole-club reading comes later; until then, only the coaches can see it. Every choice can be revoked.

  • Rides, analytics, routes and wellbeing, separately.
  • Coach only, per member; whole-club reading is next.
  • Read-only coach views, with a record of who looked.

Yours to keep

Your data. Your files. Your call.

Velomine runs on a server your club controls, or as a managed cloud tenant with its own database. Your data stays yours; in cloud we hold it only to run your club's instance.

01

One club, one database

One PostgreSQL database per club and one file namespace, self-hosted or in the cloud. That is the whole stack.

02

Every original file

FIT, GPX and TCX files stay exactly as uploaded, ready to be read again when the parser improves.

03

Export or delete

Download everything as a ZIP, including every ride file. Or delete the account and all of it; backups expire within 90 days.
