For the coach

One screen for the squad.

See who rode, how much load they carried and who is ready — before Sunday. A rider’s data appears only after they share it with you, and every look is logged.

  • Last ride, distance and seven- and six-week load for every athlete.
  • Readiness on the athlete page, from load, form and the daily check-in.
  • Read-only views, revocable by the rider, with a record of who looked.
  • Shared scores and RPE, never notes.

No workout plans or comments yet. Velomine is analysis and coach review today.

For the rider

Record it. Keep it. See all of it.

Record on Android with the screen off and a heart-rate strap, or upload a FIT, GPX or TCX file from any bike computer. Route on the map, a chart for every stream, laps, zones, TSS, the power curve, bikes and parts, goals and readiness.

  • Every stream on one page, with the map following the chart.
  • Bikes, parts and wear, so a chain is replaced before it fails.
  • Your files stay yours: export everything, delete everything.

For the club

The club owns the record.

Every club’s data kept apart. Members join with a code and an admin approves them. Coaches see only what each rider shares. Run it on your own server, or in our managed cloud.

01

Every club kept apart

Every request is limited to the club it belongs to, and tests prove one club can never see another club’s data.

02

Export or delete

Download everything as a ZIP, including every original file. Delete the account and all of it goes; backups expire within 90 days.

03

Self-host or cloud

The same software on the club’s server, or in our managed cloud in the EU. Same features, same export, same ownership.

04

Admin and single sign-on

Join with a code and an admin’s approval. Roles for club admin, coach and member, sign-in with the accounts the club already uses — Google, Microsoft or its own identity provider — plus optional two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, and a list of every device you are signed in on.
