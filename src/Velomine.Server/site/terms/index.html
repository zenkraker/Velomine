Terms

The agreement, in plain language.

What a club gets, what it pays, what it owns, and what happens if either side wants to stop.

The service

Velomine is cycling training software for clubs and teams. A club can run it on its own server (self-hosted) or use a managed cloud instance with its own database. The features are the same; only the operator differs. The current feature list is on the status page.

Your club account

The club is the customer. A club administrator manages the account, approves members and decides who is a coach. Members own their own data and keep their own sign-in. One account belongs to one club database; moving to another club means an account there.

Fees

  • One plan. Every feature is included for every member; there is no feature paywall.
  • One activation fee, then one yearly fee per club. Never per member.
  • The amount for your club's size band is published before you commit, and comparable clubs pay the same. While Velomine is in early development, the first clubs agree it openly before signing.
  • Cloud includes hosting, a secure connection, updates, monitoring and backups. A self-hosted club pays for the licence and runs its own server, domain and backups.
  • Taxes are added where they apply. Invoices are issued annually in advance.

If an invoice is late

We send a reminder and a grace period. After that, writes may be suspended, but the club and its members keep read access and export. Data is not deleted for a long notice period, and export always remains available. We do not hold data hostage for a payment.

Member data

Members own their data. The club decides who may see it through the sharing settings, and a member can revoke a choice at any time. On the cloud plan, the club is the controller and Velomine is the processor under the signed DPA. On the self-hosted plan, the club runs the instance and is the controller. Export and deletion are available in the app; backups expire within 90 days.

Acceptable use

  • Do not break the law, infringe rights, or upload malicious files.
  • Do not try to access another club's database, another member's account, or the service's infrastructure.
  • Do not scrape, overload or resell the service without an agreement.
  • Club administrators are responsible for who they approve and for their own club's content.

Availability and support

We aim for a reliable service but do not promise uninterrupted availability. Support is during business hours, with the response times in the club agreement; there is no fake 24/7 promise. Maintenance that may take more than a minute is announced to club administrators. Backups are for recovery, not a substitute for a member's own export.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, Velomine is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss that a backup and export could have prevented. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be limited by law. The signed club agreement states the governing law and jurisdiction.

Ending the agreement

Either side can end the agreement at the renewal date with the notice in the club agreement. The club can export everything before the account closes. After closure, the database, files and derived data are deleted; backups expire within 90 days. A self-hosted club keeps its own installation and data.

Open source

Velomine is open source under AGPL-3.0; the licence page explains what that allows. The source code and the full product values are in the Velomine repository.

Changes

We version these terms. A material change is announced to club administrators before it takes effect, and the date below changes. The version shown when you use the service applies.
