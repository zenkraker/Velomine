Demo

Try a real club.

Not a tour. A seeded club with three months of rides, three accounts, and a nightly reset. Upload your own GPX and see what breaks.

Sign in as anyone.

Three accounts share one seeded club. Open the same ride from the coach side and the rider side and compare what each of them can see.

demo-admin@velomine.eu Club admin: members, settings, approval
demo-coach@velomine.eu Coach: the squad, shared rides, power curve
demo-rider@velomine.eu Rider: rides, map, charts, laps, bikes, goals
Velomine-demo-1 Password, shared by all three accounts

Resets every night at 03:00 UTC

  1. Sign in as the coach and open the squad. Note who shares rides, analytics and wellbeing.
  2. Sign in as the rider and open a ride. Move over a chart and watch the map follow.
  3. Upload your own GPX and find it in the list.
  4. Tell me what broke, what was missing, and what you expected.

Before you start

Everything here is example data. Files you upload are deleted at the next reset, so do not put anything personal in it.
