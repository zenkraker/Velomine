Where it runs

Self-hosted or in the cloud.

Same Velomine, same data ownership. Choose where the server lives and who keeps it running.

Self-hosted

  • Installed on a server your club controls
  • The database and ride files never leave your infrastructure
  • You provide the machine, domain and backups
  • Activation covers the setup and go-live

Cloud

  • A managed instance with its own database
  • We run the app, keep the connection secure, and handle updates and backups
  • New-club enrollment is done with you personally while onboarding is automated
  • Your data is still yours, and you can leave at any time

Questions

A few honest answers.

How does the pricing work?

A one-time activation fee, then a yearly fee per club, never per member. The amount is published for your club's size band before you commit, and comparable clubs pay the same. There is no feature paywall.

Do I need to be technical?

Self-hosted: activation covers the setup; the club provides the machine and backups. Cloud: nothing to run. Either way you do not have to build the app.

Is there a hosted version?

Yes. A managed instance with its own database, alongside self-hosting. We run the app, updates and backups. New-club enrollment is done with you personally while the onboarding automation is finished.

Can I move my data?

Yes. Download a ZIP with your profile, bikes, goals, rides and every original ride file. You can also delete the account and everything in it; backups expire within 90 days. Strava import is not here yet — bring FIT, GPX or TCX today; the solo plan opens when import ships.

What is still missing?

Whole-club sharing (needs a member-facing club view), photos, share links, privacy zones, per-stream privacy, cycle tracking, the iOS recorder, speed, cadence and power sensors, connectors, Strava and Garmin import, two-factor sign-in, passkeys and the Italian pages. The status page lists every gap.

How do I start?

Get in touch with the size band of your club and whether you prefer self-hosted or cloud. We confirm the published fee and start onboarding.
