Questions

A few honest answers.

How does the pricing work?

A one-time activation fee, then a yearly fee per club, never per member. The amount is published for your club's size band before you commit, and comparable clubs pay the same. There is no feature paywall.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Choose self-hosted or cloud. Activation covers the setup, and the annual fee includes support for your club admins. Self-hosting still needs a machine and backups, but you do not have to build the app.

Is there a hosted version?

Yes. A managed cloud tenant is one of the two options, alongside self-hosting. Every club gets its own database, and we run the app, updates and backups. Both keep your data yours.

Can I move my data?

Yes. Download a ZIP with your profile, bikes, goals, rides and every original ride file. You can also delete the account and everything in it; backups expire within 90 days.

What is still missing?

Whole-club sharing (needs a member-facing club view), photos, share links, privacy zones, cycle tracking, the iOS recorder, speed, cadence and power sensors, connectors and some account features. The status page tracks all of it.

How do I start?

Get in touch with the size band of your club and whether you prefer self-hosted or cloud. We confirm the published fee and start onboarding.
