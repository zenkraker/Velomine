Questions

A few honest answers.

How does the pricing work?

A one-time activation fee, then a yearly fee per member. The exact amounts depend on the size of your club and whether you choose on-premise or cloud. There is no feature paywall.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Choose on-premise or cloud. Activation covers the setup, and the annual fee includes support for your admins. On-premise still needs a machine and backups, but you do not have to build the app.

Is there a hosted version?

Yes. A dedicated cloud instance is one of the two options, alongside on-premise. Both run the same Velomine and both keep your data yours.

Can I move my data?

Yes. Download a ZIP with your profile, bikes, goals, rides and every original ride file. You can also delete the account and everything in it.

What is still missing?

Share links, privacy zones, cycle tracking, photos, laps, the iOS recorder, power and cadence sensors, connectors and some account features. The status page tracks all of it.

How do I get a quote?

Get in touch with the size of your club and whether you prefer on-premise or cloud. That is enough to start.
